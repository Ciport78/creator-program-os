# 4. Brief template

**Principle:** tell the creator what must be true, not how to say it. Their voice is why their audience trusts them.

One page. If it does not fit on one page, it is a script, not a brief.

---

## Creator brief: [Campaign name]

**Creator:** [Name, handle, platform]
**Segment:** Creators / Marketers / Developers
**Deliverable:** [Format, length, placement]
**Draft due:** [Date] · **Live date:** [Date]

### 1. The one thing
> What should the viewer believe or do after watching? One sentence.

*Example: "This cut my editing time in half, and the output is better than what I made by hand."*

### 2. Why you
Why we chose this creator and the angle we think fits their audience.

### 3. Product focus
- **Feature:** [the one feature this piece focuses on]
- **Show, don't tell:** the moment in the workflow that should appear on screen
- **Access provided:** [account tier, credits, early access details]

### 4. Must include
- [ ] Hands-on demo of the product (screen or real output)
- [ ] Tracked link: `[link]` and code: `[CODE]` mentioned verbally and in description
- [ ] Clear sponsorship disclosure
- [ ] Call to action: [e.g. "Try it free with my link"]

### 5. Please avoid
- Claims we cannot support (e.g. "perfect", "indistinguishable from human" without a demo)
- Financial or performance promises (e.g. returns, guaranteed results)
- Comparing competitors by name
- Reading this brief on camera

### 6. Creative freedom
Format, tone, structure, humour, and opinion are yours. Honest critique of limitations is welcome. It builds trust.

### 7. Reference
- 1 to 2 examples of content that worked (any brand), and why
- Key messages to draw from (not to read verbatim)

### 8. Approval
- One draft review round, feedback within 48 hours
- Feedback limited to factual accuracy, must-includes and brand safety

---

## Feedback rules for the program team

- **Fast:** feedback within 48 hours, or the draft is approved by default
- **Decisive:** one consolidated feedback note, never three people commenting separately
- **Scoped:** only flag accuracy, must-includes and safety issues
- **Specific:** "Show the output at 0:45 before explaining the settings" beats "make it more engaging"
- **Never:** rewrite the creator's script or change their tone
