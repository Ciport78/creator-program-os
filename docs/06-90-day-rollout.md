# 6. 90-day rollout

## Days 1 to 30: foundations and first tests

**Build**
- Audit organic creator mentions: who already talks about the product, with what results
- Set up tracking: UTM convention, code system, survey field, creator tracker
- Align with Data on how creator activity feeds attribution and the halo model
- Draft contract templates and usage-rights pricing with Legal
- Publish brief template and approval workflow

**Test**
- Sign 10 to 15 T2 and T3 creators across all 3 segments
- Mix deal types: mostly performance hybrids, a few flat fees for comparison
- Launch an affiliate program for existing advocates

**Decision by day 30:** which segment shows the lowest early cost per tracked sign-up.

## Days 31 to 60: learn and double down

**Build**
- First monthly creator scorecard
- Rate card v1 based on actual deals
- Whitelisting workflow with the paid team

**Test**
- Rebook top 30% of creators with iterated briefs
- Add 10 to 15 new creators in the best-performing segment
- First holdout or time-based lift test on a larger campaign
- Whitelist the top 3 creator assets in paid media

**Decision by day 60:** continue / pause / scale calls on every creator from wave 1.

## Days 61 to 90: launch moment and scale plan

**Build**
- Sourcing pipeline running weekly with clear stage owners
- Quarterly program report: what worked, by segment, tier and deal type

**Test**
- Plan a coordinated creator wave around a product launch: T1 anchors on launch day, T2 and T3 tutorials in the following 2 weeks
- Offer first ambassador contracts to the top 2 to 3 performers

**Decision by day 90:** 12-month program plan and budget recommendation, backed by incrementality data.

## Success at day 90

- Tracking and reporting live and trusted by Data and Paid
- 25+ creators tested, with clear continue / pause / scale outcomes
- A proven segment and format with a cost per incremental paid user that justifies scaling
- A repeatable system that a second team member could run from this playbook
