# 1. Strategy and creator tiers

## Program goals (first 12 months)

| Goal | Primary KPI | Secondary KPI |
|------|-------------|---------------|
| Drive new paid users | Incremental paid sign-ups | Cost per incremental paid user |
| Accelerate product launches | Launch-week trial lift vs baseline | Share of voice in launch week |
| Build a durable creator bench | Creators with 2+ profitable campaigns | Ambassador retention rate |
| Feed paid media | Creator assets whitelisted | Paid CPA on creator assets vs brand assets |

## Audience segments

Most products sell to more than one buyer. Map each segment before sourcing a single creator, because each needs its own creator mix, brief and KPI.

| Segment (example) | Who they follow | Hook that converts | Best platforms | Conversion event |
|-------------------|-----------------|--------------------|----------------|------------------|
| **End users / consumers** | Lifestyle, how-to and review creators | Save time, look better, get results fast | YouTube, TikTok, Reels | Free sign-up, then paid upgrade |
| **Professionals / teams** | Practitioners, operators, newsletter writers | Do more with less, proven workflow | X threads, YouTube deep dives, newsletters, podcasts | Team trial, demo request |
| **Builders / developers** | Technical educators, build-in-public accounts | Fast path to a working result | YouTube builds, X, livestreams, GitHub-adjacent communities | API key, first paid usage |
| **Traders / power users** (crypto) | Analysts, alpha callers, protocol educators | Edge, yield, early access | X, Telegram, YouTube | Wallet connect, first trade or deposit |

Rename and trim the segments to fit your product. Three is usually enough.

## Creator tiers

| Tier | Typical reach | Role in the program | Default deal | Share of creator budget |
|------|---------------|---------------------|--------------|-------------------------|
| **T1: Anchors** | 1M+ or category-defining | Launch moments, credibility, halo | Flat fee or ambassador with usage rights | 30 to 35% |
| **T2: Workhorses** | 100K to 1M | Consistent, measurable conversion | Performance hybrid | 40 to 45% |
| **T3: Specialists** | 10K to 100K, high-trust niche | Deep tutorials, niche segments, testing | Low flat fee + affiliate | 15 to 20% |
| **T4: Community** | Under 10K, power users | Volume, UGC, organic advocacy | Affiliate, product credits, recognition | 5% plus tooling |

**Rule of thumb:** T2 and T3 prove the channel. T1 accelerates what already works. Do not lead with T1 before the funnel, landing pages and tracking are validated.

## Budget allocation logic

- **70% proven:** creators and formats with demonstrated incremental return
- **20% scaling tests:** new creators in proven segments, new formats with proven creators
- **10% experiments:** new platforms, new segments, new deal structures

Reallocate monthly based on the scorecard in [Measurement](05-measurement.md).

## Content pillars

1. **Workflow demos:** "How I do [job] in 20 minutes with [product]"
2. **Before / after:** the result without the product vs with it
3. **Build-alongs:** "I built [thing] in one afternoon"
4. **Launch reactions:** first-look content on new models and features within 48 hours of release
5. **Use-case stories:** a creator's real business result, told by them

## Where this program should not compete

- Generic "top 10 tools" listicles with no hands-on usage
- Creators whose audience is mostly other affiliates and promoters
- One-off viral moments with no repeatable format
