# 2. Sourcing and vetting

## Sourcing pipeline

Start where intent already exists, then widen.

| Source | Why it matters | Method |
|--------|----------------|--------|
| **Organic mentions** | Creators already using the product convert best | Social listening on product names, category keywords, competitor mentions |
| **Existing users** | Power users with audiences | Match high-usage accounts against social handles (with consent) |
| **Competitor creators** | Proven appetite for the category | Track creators promoting competing products in your category |
| **Adjacent categories** | Same audience, different tool | Video editing, podcast production, no-code and AI builder creators |
| **Inbound applications** | Scales the long tail | Public creator program page with a short application form |
| **Agencies and networks** | Speed for T1, regional reach | Use selectively, benchmark rates against direct deals |

Pipeline stages: `Identified → Vetted → Outreached → Negotiating → Contracted → Live → Reviewed`

## Vetting scorecard

Score every creator 1 to 5 on each dimension before any outreach. Minimum to proceed: **18 / 30**, and no score below 2 on Audience fit or Brand safety.

| Dimension | Weight | What "5" looks like |
|-----------|--------|---------------------|
| **Audience fit** | x2 | Audience matches one of your segments closely, by topic, geography and comments |
| **Engagement quality** | x1 | Comments ask "how did you do this?", not just emojis |
| **Conversion history** | x1 | Visible track record driving sign-ups for software or tools |
| **Content craft** | x1 | Clear demos, good audio, strong hooks |
| **Brand safety** | x1 | No history of misleading claims, deepfake misuse or controversy |

*Weighted total out of 30 (Audience fit counts twice).*

## Red flags (automatic pass)

- Engagement spikes inconsistent with follower growth
- Comment sections dominated by bots or generic replies
- Promotes 5+ competing AI tools per month with no hands-on usage
- Content that conflicts with your brand or safety policies (for crypto: undisclosed paid shills, pump-and-dump history)
- Unwilling to disclose sponsored content properly

## Rate benchmarking

Maintain an internal rate card updated after every deal:

- Price per **integration**, per **dedicated video**, per **short**, per **thread**
- Effective **CPM** (fee ÷ expected views × 1,000) and **cost per incremental sign-up** after the campaign
- Tag every rate by tier, platform, segment and region

After 20+ deals, use the benchmark to anchor every negotiation.

## Outreach

- Lead with **why them specifically**: reference a video, show you watched it
- Offer product access first for T3 and T4, before any money conversation
- One clear ask, one clear next step
- Follow up once after 5 working days, then park the lead
