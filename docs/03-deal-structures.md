# 3. Deal structures

Match the deal to what we are buying. Awareness, conversion and long-term advocacy are priced differently.

## Overview

| Structure | Best for | Risk sits with | Use when |
|-----------|----------|----------------|----------|
| **Flat fee** | Launches, credibility, T1 | Brand | Reach and timing matter more than direct conversion |
| **Performance hybrid** | Repeatable conversion, T2 and T3 | Shared | We want aligned incentives and have reliable tracking |
| **Ambassador** | Long-term advocacy, proven creators | Shared | A creator has delivered 2+ profitable campaigns |
| **Affiliate only** | Long tail, T4 | Creator | Volume and organic advocacy at low cost |

## 1. Flat fee

Fixed payment for defined deliverables.

- **Define:** format, length, integration placement (pre-roll, mid-roll, dedicated), post date, number of revisions
- **Protect:** draft review, removal rights for factual errors, minimum live period
- **Negotiation levers:** bundle formats (long video + 2 shorts), add usage rights instead of raising fee, offer repeat-deal commitment for a lower unit price

## 2. Performance hybrid

Lower base fee plus a variable component tied to outcomes.

**Example structure (illustrative):**
- Base fee: 50 to 60% of the creator's flat rate
- Variable: fixed bonus per paid sign-up or revenue share on first-year subscription value
- Bonus cap to keep budget predictable
- Accelerator: higher payout above an agreed threshold

**Why creators accept it:** total upside exceeds their flat rate when the content works.
**Why it works for the brand:** spend tracks results, and top performers self-select into more volume.

**Requirements before using it:** unique code + tracked link, an attribution window agreed in writing (e.g. 30 days), and monthly reporting shared with the creator.

## 3. Ambassador

3 to 12 month partnership for creators with proven results.

- **Includes:** monthly content commitment, early access to new models and features, launch-day priority, feedback channel to product
- **Pay:** monthly retainer + performance bonus
- **Exclusivity:** category exclusivity only, priced explicitly, never assumed
- **Review:** quarterly performance check with a clear exit clause

## 4. Affiliate

Commission on referred paid users. No base fee.

- Self-serve sign-up, instant tracked link and code
- Tiered commissions that rise with volume
- Monthly leaderboard and recognition for the top contributors
- Best route for converting organic advocates into a measured channel

## Usage rights and whitelisting

Always negotiate separately and price explicitly:

| Right | Typical term | Notes |
|-------|--------------|-------|
| Organic repost on brand channels | 6 to 12 months | Low cost, often bundled |
| Paid usage (ads using creator content) | 3 to 6 months | Price as % uplift on base fee |
| Whitelisting (ads run from creator's handle) | 30 to 90 days, renewable | Highest performing paid format, requires trust and approval process |
| Edit rights (cut-downs, translations) | Match paid usage term | Important for multilingual repurposing |

## Contract checklist (with Legal)

- [ ] Deliverables, dates, revision rounds
- [ ] FTC / ASA disclosure requirements
- [ ] Usage rights, duration, territories
- [ ] Exclusivity scope (if any)
- [ ] Payment terms and performance calculation method
- [ ] Brand safety and content removal clauses
- [ ] Category-specific compliance (e.g. financial promotion rules for crypto, consent for AI-generated likeness)
